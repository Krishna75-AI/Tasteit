Who We Are

Welcome to Tasteit, your personal recipe finder! Our website helps users discover different recipes from around the world. Whether you are looking for something new to cook or searching for your favourite dish, Tasteit makes finding recipes simple and easy.

Our Project

Tasteit is a Recipe Finder website developed using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. We use TheMealDB API to fetch recipes and display their ingredients, instructions and cooking details.
